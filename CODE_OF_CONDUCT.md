# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1
in spirit, with one explicit amendment for medical-adjacent work.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background, with the additional understanding that some participants may be
clinicians or researchers working in diabetes care. Be patient with questions that touch
on clinical context.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming. Assume good faith. Use precise language; this is a research codebase.
- Cite sources. Every quoted result or borrowed idea should reference its origin.
- Distinguish reconstruction from invention. This is a re-implementation of a published
  method; do not present modifications of the method as if they were the published method
  without saying so.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing participant data, even accidentally. The data is gitignored for a reason.
- Claiming this is "Google's GlucoFM" or "the authors' GlucoFM". It is an independent
  reconstruction.
- Promoting medical claims unsupported by the published evidence. The model is a
  research prototype, not a medical device.

## Enforcement

Project maintainer: Stephane Fourdrinier (`@sfourdrinier` on GitHub). Report incidents via
GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting flow.
